Our Story

Where the Ocean Meets Family Warmth

Nestled right on the shores of Bentota Beach, Sisi's Place is a family-run boutique retreat that offers something rare — the genuine warmth of a home combined with the comfort of a top-rated hotel. Your host, Sisi, and his family treat every guest like a personal friend.

Wake up to the sound of waves, enjoy a freshly made breakfast with tropical fruits, and let our family help you discover the best of southern Sri Lanka — from hidden beaches to ancient temples. With a rating of 9.9 and 5-star reviews, we pride ourselves on going above and beyond.
